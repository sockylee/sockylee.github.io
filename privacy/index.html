一、適用範圍

本政策適用於 李碩祈（Shuo Chi Lee）以個人開發者身分於 Apple App Store 與 Google Play 發行的所有應用程式（以下稱「本 App」）。各 App 若有額外的資料處理方式，會在本頁下方的「各 App 補充說明」中個別列出。

使用本 App 即表示您已閱讀並同意本政策。若您不同意，請停止使用並移除本 App。

二、我們不收集的資料

  • 不會將您在 App 內輸入的內容上傳到開發者自有的伺服器。
  • 不會出售、出租或交換您的任何資料給第三方。

三、儲存在您裝置上的資料

本 App 的設定與您輸入的內容（例如查詢紀錄、偏好設定、購買狀態快取）以 Core Data、UserDefaults（iOS）或 Room、DataStore（Android）儲存在您的裝置本機。這些資料不會傳送給開發者，並會在您刪除 App 時一併移除。

六、兒童隱私

本 App 並非以兒童為主要對象，亦不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。若您認為有未成年人在未經監護人同意下提供了資料，請來信告知，我們會儘速處理。

由於資料儲存在您的裝置本機，刪除 App 即等同刪除全部資料。第三方服務（如 Google、Apple）所保留的資料，請依其各自政策向該公司申請。

本政策如有修改，將直接更新本頁面並修改頁首的「最後更新」日期。重大變更會在 App 更新說明中一併告知。

1. Scope

This policy applies to all applications published by Shuo Chi Lee as an individual developer on the Apple App Store and Google Play (the "App"). App-specific practices, if any, are listed under "App-specific notes" below.

By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using and delete the App.

2. What we do not collect

  • The App requires no account and does not collect your name, phone number, email address or any other directly identifying information.
  • We do not access your contacts, photo library, messages or call history, unless the App explicitly requests it with your permission (see the app-specific notes).
  • Content you enter in the App is never uploaded to servers owned by the developer.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your data.

3. Data stored on your device

Settings and the content you enter (search history, preferences, cached purchase state) are stored locally using Core Data or UserDefaults (iOS), or Room or DataStore (Android). This data is never sent to the developer and is removed when you delete the App.

5. Advertising and tracking

Apps that include advertising display ads served by Google AdMob. Ad content is neither provided nor endorsed by the developer.

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): as required by Apple, the App presents a tracking permission prompt. You may decline — ads are still shown but become non-personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA and similar regions (GDPR): consent is collected through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), and the App provides an "Ad privacy settings" entry so you can revisit your choice at any time.
  • Removing ads: where the App offers a "Remove Ads" purchase or a subscription that includes it, ads are removed and the ad SDK is not initialized at all, so none of the ad-related processing above takes place.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a minor has provided data without a guardian's consent, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

7. Retention and deletion

Because data is stored locally on your device, deleting the App deletes all of it. For data retained by third parties such as Google or Apple, please refer to their respective policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU, CCPA in California, PDPA in Taiwan) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. As the developer holds no data that identifies you, deleting the App is usually sufficient; for anything further, please contact us.

Changes are published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.

App-specific notes

Taiwan Lottery Live（台彩開獎速報）

iOS Android Self-published
  • Data source: public draw numbers are read from a Google Firebase Realtime Database in a read-only manner; nothing about you is uploaded.
  • Camera: used solely to scan your paper lottery ticket. Recognition happens entirely on your device (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on-device text recognition on Android); images are never stored or uploaded. Declining only disables scanning.
  • Local storage: draw data, the numbers you enter and your scanned tickets are kept on your device.
  • Advertising: the free version shows AdMob banners on the Latest, History and Draw Detail screens.
  • In-app purchases: the "Pro" subscription (unlocks ticket checking and My Tickets, and includes an ad-free experience) and the one-time "Remove Ads" item — handled by Apple on iOS and by Google Play Billing on Android.
  • Advertising ID on Android: the Android app declares the AD_ID permission so AdMob can use the Android advertising ID; you can delete or reset it in Android Settings → Google → Ads. After purchasing Remove Ads or Pro, the ad SDK is not initialized.
  • Android data safety: the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device or other IDs, approximate location (derived from IP), app interactions and crash logs for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention; the developer does not receive this data.
  • Note: this is an unofficial tool; official announcements by Taiwan Lottery always prevail.

MyMoney（我的錢錢）

iOS / iPadOS / macOS Self-published
  • Your financial data: transactions, accounts, budgets, categories, notes, tags and receipt photos are stored on your device with Core Data and, when iCloud is on, synced via Apple CloudKit to your own private iCloud database. The developer runs no servers and cannot access any of your data.
  • Shared ledgers: if you choose to share a ledger, its contents are shared through iCloud with the participants you invite, under Apple's iCloud terms. You can stop sharing or remove participants at any time.
  • Deleting data: deleting the app removes local data; the iCloud copy can be deleted in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • Stock quotes (Max plan): when refreshing prices the app sends only the ticker symbol to Yahoo Finance's public quote service; no personal or financial data is included. Yahoo may log the connecting IP address under its privacy policy.
  • Face ID: the app lock uses Apple's on-device LocalAuthentication; the app never receives biometric data.
  • Photos: only the single photo you pick through the system photo picker is read, when you attach a receipt.
  • Notifications: budget and recurring-transaction reminders are local notifications.
  • In-app purchases: Pro / Max auto-renewing subscriptions are processed by Apple; the developer never receives your payment details.
  • No ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics SDKs.
