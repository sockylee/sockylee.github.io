找間咖啡館 · 支援

在台灣找一間有插座、能久坐的咖啡廳

「找間咖啡館」收錄全台灣的咖啡廳資料，讓你用地圖或清單，依插座、不限時、WiFi、安靜程度等條件，找到真正適合帶筆電久坐的店。資料下載後存在手機裡，沒網路也查得到。

App 免費使用：前 7 天完全沒有廣告，之後畫面底部會出現一則橫幅；也可以一次性購買「移除廣告」永久關掉。

常見問題

為什麼需要定位權限？可以不給嗎？

定位只用來把地圖置中在你附近，以及計算你與各咖啡廳的距離。計算全部在手機上完成，位置資料不會傳送到任何地方，也不會提供給廣告。

可以拒絕。拒絕後 App 仍可正常使用，只是地圖會以台北市中心為預設位置，距離會顯示「無法計算距離」。

資料多久更新一次？

App 每天會自動檢查並更新一次。你也可以隨時到「設定 → 立即更新資料」手動更新。超過 15 天沒有再出現的資料會自動清除。

某家店的資訊有誤（已歇業、沒有插座、地址不對）怎麼辦？

咖啡廳資料來自 Cafe Nomad，是由社群共同編輯維護的公開資料庫，我們不自行編輯內容。

最直接的更正方式是到 Cafe Nomad 網站上修改；也歡迎透過下方信箱告訴我們，我們會協助反映。

為什麼有些店沒有評分，或評分是 0？

評分同樣來自 Cafe Nomad 的社群投票。還沒有人評分的店家會顯示 0。設定「最低評分」篩選時，這些店會被濾掉，這是預期行為。

為什麼搜尋不到某個城市的店？

請先確認「設定 → 顯示城市」裡該縣市的開關是開啟的，並檢查目前的篩選條件是否過於嚴格（地圖與清單右上角會顯示目前生效的條件數量）。

為什麼會有廣告？什麼時候開始出現？

App 是免費的，廣告是維持它繼續更新的唯一來源。從你第一次開啟起有 7 天免費體驗期，期間完全沒有廣告（廣告元件根本不會啟動）。體驗期結束後，畫面底部會出現一則橫幅。

App 內只有這一種廣告 —— 沒有插頁式、影片或獎勵式廣告，也不會蓋住分頁列或列表的最後一筆。設定頁可以看到體驗期還剩幾天。

廣告會根據我的個人資料投放嗎？

不會。本 App 向 Google AdMob 送出的每一次廣告請求都標記為非個人化，不會依你的個資或跨 App 行為決定內容。

如果你覺得廣告和所在地區有關，那是 Google 依連線的 IP 位址判斷的大略區域（國家／城市層級），與 App 的定位權限無關 —— 你的 GPS 位置從來不會提供給廣告。詳見隱私權政策。

可以把廣告移除嗎？

可以。到「設定 → 移除廣告」完成一次性購買即可永久移除。這不是訂閱，不會自動續扣，只付一次。價格以 App 內顯示的為準。

若價格顯示「暫時無法取得」，通常是暫時連不上商店服務，稍後再開一次設定頁即可；仍然不行的話請來信告訴我們。

我換了手機／重新安裝，買過的「移除廣告」不見了

用原本購買時的 Apple ID／Google 帳號登入裝置，然後到「設定 → 恢復購買」。權益取回後廣告會立刻消失。

若仍取不回來，請來信並附上購買日期與收據（App Store／Google Play 的購買通知信），我們會協助處理。

App 會不會蒐集我的資料？

我們不蒐集。沒有帳號、沒有分析工具、沒有崩潰回報服務、沒有跨 App 追蹤，我們也沒有任何自營伺服器。

顯示廣告時，Google 會取得投放所需的有限技術資訊（IP 位址、裝置基本資訊等），但不與你的身分連結。iOS本 App 不會跳出「App 追蹤透明度」要求，因此不會取得 IDFA。完整說明見隱私權政策。

Find a Cafe · Support

Find a cafe in Taiwan with power outlets where you can actually sit and work

Find a Cafe covers cafes all across Taiwan. Browse by map or list and filter by power outlets, time limits, wifi and quietness to find somewhere you can settle in with a laptop. Data is cached on your phone, so it works offline.

The app is free: no ads for the first 7 days, after which a single banner appears at the bottom of the screen. A one-time purchase removes it for good.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the app need my location? Can I say no?

Location is used only to centre the map near you and to calculate how far each cafe is. All of it happens on your device — your location is never sent anywhere, and never given to the ad service.

You can decline. The app still works; the map just defaults to central Taipei and distances show “Distance unavailable”.

How often is the data updated?

The app checks for updates once a day automatically. You can also refresh manually from Settings → Update now. Entries that stop appearing for more than 15 days are cleared automatically.

A cafe's details are wrong (closed down, no outlets, wrong address). What now?

Cafe data comes from Cafe Nomad, a community-maintained open database. We do not edit the entries ourselves.

The most direct fix is to correct it on the Cafe Nomad site. You're also welcome to email us and we'll help pass it along.

Why do some cafes have no ratings, or a rating of 0?

Ratings are crowd-sourced through Cafe Nomad. A cafe nobody has rated yet shows 0. Those cafes are filtered out when you set a minimum rating — that's expected.

Why can't I find cafes in a particular city?

Check that the city is switched on under Settings → Cities shown, and that your filters aren't too strict. The number of active filters is shown in the top-right of the Map and List screens.

Why are there ads, and when do they start?

The app is free, and ads are what keep it maintained. You get a 7-day free trial from the moment you first open it, with no ads at all — the ad component isn't even started. After that, one banner appears at the bottom of the screen.

That's the only ad format in the app: no interstitials, no video, no rewarded ads, and it never covers the tab bar or the last row of a list. Settings shows how many trial days remain.

Are ads targeted using my personal data?

No. Every ad request the app sends to Google AdMob is flagged as non-personalised, so your personal data and cross-app behaviour are not used to pick the ad.

If an ad looks regionally relevant, that's Google inferring a rough area (country/city level) from your IP address. It has nothing to do with the app's location permission — your GPS location is never given to the ad service. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I remove the ads?

Yes. Settings → Remove ads is a one-time purchase that removes them permanently. It is not a subscription and nothing auto-renews. The price shown in the app is authoritative.

If the price reads “Temporarily unavailable”, the store service is usually just unreachable for a moment — reopen Settings shortly after. If it persists, email us.

I changed phones or reinstalled, and my “Remove ads” purchase is gone

Sign in with the same Apple ID / Google account you bought it with, then tap Settings → Restore purchase. Ads disappear as soon as the entitlement is restored.

If it still doesn't come back, email us with the purchase date and your receipt (the confirmation mail from the App Store / Google Play) and we'll sort it out.

Does the app collect my data?

We collect nothing. No account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no cross-app tracking, and no servers of our own.

While ads are shown, Google receives the limited technical data needed to serve them (IP address, basic device info), not linked to your identity. iOSThe app never shows an App Tracking Transparency prompt, so it does not obtain the IDFA. Full details in the Privacy Policy.
