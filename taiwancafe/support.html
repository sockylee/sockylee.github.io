找間咖啡館 · 支援

在台灣找一間有插座、能久坐的咖啡廳

「找間咖啡館」收錄全台灣的咖啡廳資料，讓你用地圖或清單，依插座、不限時、WiFi、安靜程度等條件，找到真正適合帶筆電久坐的店。資料下載後存在手機裡，沒網路也查得到。

常見問題

為什麼需要定位權限？可以不給嗎？

定位只用來把地圖置中在你附近，以及計算你與各咖啡廳的距離。計算全部在手機上完成，位置資料不會傳送到任何地方。

可以拒絕。拒絕後 App 仍可正常使用，只是地圖會以台北市中心為預設位置，距離會顯示「無法計算距離」。

資料多久更新一次？

App 每天會自動檢查並更新一次。你也可以隨時到「設定 → 立即更新資料」手動更新。超過 15 天沒有再出現的資料會自動清除。

某家店的資訊有誤（已歇業、沒有插座、地址不對）怎麼辦？

咖啡廳資料來自 Cafe Nomad，是由社群共同編輯維護的公開資料庫，我們不自行編輯內容。

最直接的更正方式是到 Cafe Nomad 網站上修改；也歡迎透過下方信箱告訴我們，我們會協助反映。

為什麼有些店沒有評分，或評分是 0？

評分同樣來自 Cafe Nomad 的社群投票。還沒有人評分的店家會顯示 0。設定「最低評分」篩選時，這些店會被濾掉，這是預期行為。

為什麼搜尋不到某個城市的店？

請先確認「設定 → 顯示城市」裡該縣市的開關是開啟的，並檢查目前的篩選條件是否過於嚴格（地圖與清單右上角會顯示目前生效的條件數量）。

App 會不會蒐集我的資料？

不會。沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析工具、沒有追蹤。詳見隱私權政策。

Find a Cafe · Support

Find a cafe in Taiwan with power outlets where you can actually sit and work

Find a Cafe covers cafes all across Taiwan. Browse by map or list and filter by power outlets, time limits, wifi and quietness to find somewhere you can settle in with a laptop. Data is cached on your phone, so it works offline.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the app need my location? Can I say no?

Location is used only to centre the map near you and to calculate how far each cafe is. All of it happens on your device — your location is never sent anywhere.

You can decline. The app still works; the map just defaults to central Taipei and distances show “Distance unavailable”.

How often is the data updated?

The app checks for updates once a day automatically. You can also refresh manually from Settings → Update now. Entries that stop appearing for more than 15 days are cleared automatically.

A cafe's details are wrong (closed down, no outlets, wrong address). What now?

Cafe data comes from Cafe Nomad, a community-maintained open database. We do not edit the entries ourselves.

The most direct fix is to correct it on the Cafe Nomad site. You're also welcome to email us and we'll help pass it along.

Why do some cafes have no ratings, or a rating of 0?

Ratings are crowd-sourced through Cafe Nomad. A cafe nobody has rated yet shows 0. Those cafes are filtered out when you set a minimum rating — that's expected.

Why can't I find cafes in a particular city?

Check that the city is switched on under Settings → Cities shown, and that your filters aren't too strict. The number of active filters is shown in the top-right of the Map and List screens.

Does the app collect my data?

No. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
