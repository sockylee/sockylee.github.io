乾淨、不打擾Clean and quiet

沒有帳號、沒有追蹤，廣告只有一則橫幅

  • 前 7 天完全沒有廣告。從第一次開啟算起的免費體驗期內，廣告元件根本不會啟動。
  • 之後只有底部一則橫幅。沒有插頁式、影片或獎勵式廣告，也不會蓋住分頁列或列表最後一筆。
  • 一律是非個人化廣告。每一次廣告請求都標記為非個人化，不依你的個資或跨 App 行為決定內容。
  • 想關掉就關掉。設定頁可一次性購買「移除廣告」永久移除，不是訂閱、不會續扣。
  • 位置留在你的手機裡。定位只用來顯示距離與地圖置中，計算全在裝置端完成，不上傳、也不提供給廣告。
  • 沒有帳號、沒有分析、沒有崩潰回報，我們也沒有任何自營伺服器。

No account, no tracking, and just one banner

  • The first 7 days are ad-free. During the free trial that starts on first launch, the ad component isn't even started.
  • After that, one bottom banner. No interstitials, no video, no rewarded ads, and it never covers the tab bar or the last row of a list.
  • Always non-personalised. Every ad request is flagged as non-personalised, so your data and cross-app behaviour are not used to pick the ad.
  • Turn it off for good. A one-time purchase in Settings removes ads permanently — not a subscription, nothing auto-renews.
  • Your location stays on your phone. It is used only for distances and centring the map, calculated on-device, never uploaded and never given to the ad service.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no servers of our own.

資料來源Where the data comes from

由社群維護的公開資料庫

咖啡廳資料來自 Cafe Nomad，是台灣的咖啡廳愛好者共同編輯的開放資料庫。評分同樣來自社群投票，我們不自行編輯內容。

發現某家店的資訊有誤，最直接的更正方式是到 Cafe Nomad 網站上修改；也歡迎透過 App 的「設定 → 問題回報」告訴我們。

A community-maintained open database

Cafe data comes from Cafe Nomad, an open database edited by cafe-goers in Taiwan. Ratings are crowd-sourced the same way, and we don't edit the entries ourselves.

If something is wrong, the most direct fix is to correct it on the Cafe Nomad site. You're also welcome to tell us through Settings → Report an issue.
