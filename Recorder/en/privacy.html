Privacy Policy

Quick Recorder · iOS · Last updated: October 26, 2026

This is the privacy policy for iOS. See the Android version.

Thank you for using Quick Recorder (the “App”; 隨手錄 in Chinese). The App is developed and published by Shuo Chi Lee, an individual developer (“we”). This policy explains how the App handles your data.

In short: we have no servers, no accounts, and no ads, analytics or tracking. Recordings stay on your device by default. They are sent directly from your device to a cloud account you authorized only when you upload them or turn on Auto backup after saving — never through us.

1. Data we don’t collect

We do not have any of the following:

  • Our own servers, member database or account system
  • Advertising, analytics, crash reporting or any tracking tools
  • Any way to collect your name, email, phone number, location or other contact information

So we cannot see your recordings, their names, which clouds you connect, or what you store in them.

3. Cloud backup (optional)

You can connect Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Synology Drive and iCloud Drive (the free version can use iCloud Drive; other clouds require Quick Recorder Pro, see section 4). This is entirely optional: recording, playback and file management work without any cloud.

  • Authorization: Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive are authorized on each service’s official sign-in page; your account and password are handled by that service and never received by the App. Synology Drive needs your NAS QuickConnect ID, account and password, which are kept only on your device and sent directly to your NAS over HTTPS.
  • Minimum access: Google Drive can access only files created by the App (drive.file); OneDrive can access only the App’s own folder (Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder). The App reads only the backup folder, never your other cloud files.
  • Upload: the recordings you choose to upload (or that are saved while Auto backup is on), with a file name made of the recording name and time.
  • List: the App lists all audio files in the backup folder (including audio files not created by the App but placed there), reading their names, sizes and modification times, to show backup status and cloud-only recordings in the Files list.
  • Download: the App downloads a file to your device only when you tap a recording that is in the cloud.
  • Rename: when you rename a backed-up recording in the App, the App renames the matching cloud file without uploading it again. If the cloud already has the same recording under the new name, the App deletes the copy with the old name so only one remains. This is the only case in which the App deletes a cloud file, and only the old copy of that same recording.
  • List cache: the cloud file list (names, sizes and modification times) is cached on your device for offline display, and deleted when you disconnect that cloud.
  • Not through us: all of this goes directly between your device and your cloud account. We have no servers and never handle or receive any content.
  • Encryption in transit: every connection uses HTTPS.
  • iCloud Drive: uses the Apple Account signed in on your device. Recordings are stored in the “隨手錄” folder of iCloud Drive (iCloud Drive › 隨手錄 in the Files app; the folder name stays the same in every language) and handled by Apple under its privacy policy. The App never receives your Apple Account information.
  • Disconnect: tap Disconnect in Settings to delete that service’s credentials, list cache and pending uploads from your device. Files already uploaded stay in your cloud account for you to manage.

Once uploaded, recordings are handled by each cloud service under its privacy policy: Dropbox, Google, Microsoft, Synology, Apple.

Google user data

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: Google Drive access is used only to upload, list, download and rename recordings created by the App (and replace the old copy when renaming) for you. It is not used for any other purpose, not transferred to anyone, and not read by us or any third party.

4. In-app purchases (Quick Recorder Pro)

  • Payment is handled by Apple: when you buy Quick Recorder Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime), payment takes place entirely in the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account, card or any payment details.
  • Purchases are verified on your device: the App uses Apple’s StoreKit to check Apple-signed purchase records on your device to decide whether Pro is on. Purchase records are not sent to us, and we have no server to receive them.
  • Offline use: the last confirmed purchase status (plan and expiry date, with no payment or personal data) is kept in the device Keychain so Pro works offline; the App Store record takes precedence once you’re online.
  • Subscriptions, cancellation, refunds and Family Sharing are handled by Apple under its terms and privacy policy. See the Terms of Use for plans and renewal.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone: used only after you tap the record button, to record what you asked for. iOS asks the first time; change it anytime in Settings › Quick Recorder › Microphone.
  • Background audio: keeps recording when you switch apps or lock the screen, and keeps playing your own recordings on the lock screen. Used only while recording or playing your recordings; the status bar shows the microphone indicator while recording.
  • Network: used only to transfer files with cloud accounts you authorized (upload, list, download, rename) and to detect when you’re offline.

The App doesn’t ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), doesn’t use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and doesn’t use location, camera, contacts or photos.

6. Third-party components

The App uses SwiftyDropbox (Dropbox’s official SDK), GoogleSignIn and Google APIs Client (Google’s official libraries), only for sign-in and file transfer when you connect those clouds. The App contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-purpose recorder and is not directed at children. We collect no personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we hold none of your data, you are fully in control: delete recordings or disconnect clouds in the App at any time, or delete the App to remove all local data. Manage files in the cloud with each cloud service.

9. Changes to this policy

If the App adds any feature that processes data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above, and mention important changes in the App Store release notes.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email socky.lee@gmail.com.
