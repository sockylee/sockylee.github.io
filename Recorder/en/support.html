Quick Recorder · Support

iOS / Android

Quick Recorder is a simple voice recorder: open it and record, with a live waveform, a built-in player, and backup to your own cloud accounts. We run no servers, so your recordings never pass through us.

Pro comes as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription (7-day free trial for first-time subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase; all three unlock the same features. Prices are shown in the app’s upgrade screen. Upgrade in Settings › Quick Recorder Pro.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Go to Settings › Quick Recorder Pro and tap Restore Purchases, using the same iOSApple Account / AndroidGoogle account you bought with.

iOSThe monthly and yearly subscriptions support Family Sharing: family members get Pro automatically when they open the app (the lifetime purchase doesn’t support Family Sharing).

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOSIn the app, Settings › Quick Recorder Pro › Manage Subscription, or iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

AndroidGoogle Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, or it will renew. You keep Pro until the period ends. Cancelling during the yearly plan’s 7-day trial means you won’t be charged. Refunds are requested from Apple or Google.

Why doesn’t Pro bought on Android work on my iPhone?

Purchases on iOS and Android are handled by Apple and Google separately and can’t be shared; the app has no accounts or servers to sync purchases. Each platform needs its own purchase.

What happens to my recordings and settings when Pro ends?
  • Local recordings and files already backed up in the cloud are never deleted.
  • Clouds other than the default stay connected but show “Paused (Pro required)” and stop uploading. The Files list still shows their recordings, but downloading is locked.
  • Unfinished uploads are kept and continue automatically when you get Pro again.
  • Auto backup after saving and Upload on Wi-Fi only keep your settings but stop applying; they work as before when you get Pro again.
  • Renaming only renames the recording on your device; cloud file names stay the same.
Where are my recordings stored?

Recordings stay on your device by default (in the app’s private storage, which other apps can’t read). They’re sent directly from your device to a cloud account you connected only when you tap Upload or turn on Auto backup after saving.

How do I back up recordings? Can it be automatic?
  • Single upload: from the Files list, uploads to every connected cloud that doesn’t have the recording yet (the free version uploads only to the default cloud; if you’re offline, connect first).
    iOSSwipe left on a recording and tap Upload, or touch and hold and choose Upload to Cloud.
    AndroidTap “⋮” next to a recording and choose Upload to cloud (swiping left deletes).
  • Auto backup (Pro): turn on Auto backup after saving in Settings › Backup to upload every recording after you save it.
  • Upload on Wi-Fi only (Pro, on by default): turn it off to allow cellular uploads. In the free version, tapping Upload uses Wi-Fi or cellular data.
  • If an upload fails, Pro retries automatically; in the free version a red exclamation mark appears — tap it and choose Retry. The cloud icons on each row show where that recording is backed up.
Why do I see recordings that are in the cloud but not on my device?

The Files list shows your local recordings together with the audio files in the backup folders of your connected clouds. Each row has one icon per connected cloud:

  • Filled, in color: the recording is on your device and in that cloud (backed up).
  • Outlined, in color: the file is only in that cloud. The row is labeled “Cloud” and has a download button.
  • Light gray: that cloud doesn’t have the file (not backed up there yet).

For a cloud-only recording (for example after changing phones, deleting the local file, or recording on another device), tap the row or the download button to download it, then play it (downloading requires Quick Recorder Pro; the free version shows a lock).

Deleting a recording in the app does not delete its cloud backups; manage cloud files with each cloud service.

When you’re offline, the list shows the cloud list from your last connection and notes when it was taken.

Google Drive: because of Google’s permission rules (the app can access only files it created), files you add to the Recorder folder from the Google Drive website or other apps aren’t visible in the app. If you add a file with the same name as one from the app, uploading from the app creates a second file with the same name (Google Drive allows duplicate names); we recommend uploading to Google Drive only from the app. Other clouds list every audio file in the backup folder.

Synology Drive won’t connect
  • Make sure QuickConnect and Synology Drive Server are enabled on your NAS and the account may use Synology Drive.
  • Accounts with 2-step verification (2FA) aren’t supported yet; use an account without 2FA.
  • Enter only the QuickConnect ID itself (for example mynas), not the full address.
OneDrive shows “unverified”, or my work account can’t authorize

Personal Microsoft accounts authorize normally. If you use a work or school account, your organization may restrict third-party apps; ask your IT administrator or use a personal account.

How do I disconnect a cloud?

In Settings, tap Disconnect in that cloud’s section; the app deletes the credentials stored on your device. You can also revoke Quick Recorder’s access in each cloud service’s account security settings. Uploaded files are not deleted.

iOSiCloud Drive follows your Apple Account settings and has no disconnect button in the app. To stop backing up to iCloud, turn off Quick Recorder in iOS Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › iCloud Drive.

Does recording stop when I switch apps or lock the screen?

No, it keeps recording.

AndroidA persistent “Recording” notification is shown while recording. Recording still works if you deny notification permission; you just won’t see the notification.

iOSThe status bar shows the microphone indicator while recording. A phone call pauses the recording; it resumes automatically when the system allows, otherwise the screen shows that recording is paused — tap Stop and save, and everything recorded so far is kept.

Can I keep listening with the screen locked or in another app?

Yes. Playback continues when you lock the screen or switch apps, and you can pause, scrub, or skip 15 seconds back or forward from the lock screen (and the notification on Android). Playback pauses for phone calls or when headphones are unplugged, and stops when you start a new recording.

Tapping record does nothing / the app asks for microphone access

iOSSettings › Quick Recorder › Microphone, then turn it on.

AndroidSettings › Apps › Quick Recorder › Permissions › Microphone › Allow.

Are my recordings kept when I change phones or delete the app?

Deleting the app also deletes its local recordings.

iOSRecordings are included in your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backup; restore that backup to your new phone to get them back.

AndroidRecordings are not included in Google’s automatic backup (system backups have a size limit that recordings easily exceed). Upload them to your cloud before changing phones.

Files already uploaded to your cloud are not affected and can be downloaded anytime.

How do I change the app language?

The app follows your device language and shows English for languages it doesn’t support.

iOSSettings › About › Language in the app opens iOS Settings for Quick Recorder, where you can choose its language.

AndroidSettings › About › Language in the app. On Android 13 or later it opens the system App languages setting; on Android 10–12 choose a language (or “Follow system”) in the app.

I want to see the tour again

Settings › About › Replay Tour (same on iOS and Android).
