Terms of Use

Solidraft · iPhone, iPad, Mac · Last updated: November 24, 2026

The App is licensed under Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). This page adds details about the Solidraft Pro subscription and what you should know when using the App.

1. Free plan and Solidraft Pro

  • Free: up to 3 projects, with sketches, modeling features, measurement, section view, and STL, OBJ, and 3MF export. Projects are stored on this device only.
  • Solidraft Pro: unlimited projects, iCloud Sync (Mac, iPad, iPhone), parametric history editing and the variable table, STEP import and export, version history (kept for 30 days), realistic rendering and AR placement, and advanced print checks (minimum wall thickness, overhangs, automatic base face). Pro features added in later versions are included while you are subscribed.

2. Plans and auto-renewal

  • Solidraft Pro is offered as a monthly and a yearly subscription. Prices are shown on the in-app purchase screen and depend on your App Store country or region.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription after purchase in your device’s Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or in the App under Settings → Plan → Manage Subscription. After canceling, you keep access until the end of the current period.
  • If a free trial is offered, any unused portion of the trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
  • On a new device or after reinstalling, tap “Restore Purchases” in the App.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

3. When a subscription ends

Your projects are never deleted. Projects beyond the free limit can still be opened and exported. iCloud Sync pauses; projects in iCloud can still be opened and downloaded to the device.

4. Your content

The models you create and the files you export belong to you. The App does not upload or use your content. Please keep backups of important projects. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for data loss caused by device failure, deletion, or sync issues.

5. Printing and dimensions

Print checks, material estimates, and measurements are for reference only. Actual print results depend on your printer, slicer settings, and material. Please verify before printing.

6. Open source

This app uses Open CASCADE Technology. Open-source components are provided under their own licenses (LGPL-2.1 with the Open CASCADE Exception 1.0). See Settings → About → Open Source Licenses in the App.
