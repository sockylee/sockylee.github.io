Canvas

How do I start my first notebook?

In the library, tap “+” to create a notebook, then use the toolbar to add text, sticky notes, shapes, images, links, or connectors. Pan with two fingers and pinch to zoom. The canvas extends in every direction; if you lose your way, tap Fit All or the minimap in the lower-left corner.

Is handwriting only available on iPad?

Yes. On iPad you can write and draw with Apple Pencil (or turn on “Draw with Finger” in the drawing toolbar). iPhone and Mac show the ink drawn on iPad but can’t edit it.

Can I export a canvas?

Yes. From the canvas’s share menu, export a PNG (current view or the whole canvas) or a PDF (the whole canvas).

Can I get back a deleted notebook?

Deleted notebooks move to Recently Deleted and are permanently removed after 7 days. With Jotverse Pro you can restore notebooks from Recently Deleted.

Recordings and transcription

How do I add a recording to a canvas?

Recording is handled by the Quick Recorder app. Share a recording from Quick Recorder to Jotverse; tap the microphone in the canvas toolbar to start Quick Recorder and drop the recording back where you were; or open an audio file directly (on Mac, you can also drag it in).

Why can’t I transcribe a recording?

Transcription requires iOS 26, iPadOS 26, or macOS 26 or later, and Speech Recognition permission. The first time you transcribe in a language, the speech model needs to download, so stay online. The free plan includes 3 transcriptions; after that, Jotverse Pro is required.

Are my recordings uploaded?

No. Transcription happens entirely on your device. Recordings and transcripts sync only between your own devices through your iCloud.

Sync and subscriptions

New notes don’t show up on my other devices?

Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Sync status appears at the bottom of the library sidebar and in Settings → iCloud. On a slow connection, it may take a few minutes.

Pro disappeared after I switched devices?

Go to Settings and tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure you are using the Apple Account you purchased with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In your device’s Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or in the App under Settings → Manage Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current period, and your notes and transcripts are never deleted.
