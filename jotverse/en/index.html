Features

Infinite canvas

Scroll in any direction and pinch to zoom. The minimap and Fit All keep you oriented.

Sticky notes and mind maps

Text, sticky notes, shapes and connectors to map out ideas and plans.

Photos and links

Photos, link cards and lists side by side on one canvas. Export as PNG or PDF.

Apple Pencil

Circle, underline and doodle right on your notes on iPad. Your ink shows on iPhone and Mac too.

Audio cards

Record with Quick Recorder and drop the recording right back where you were, or import it from the share sheet.

TranscriptionPRO

Turn recordings into text on device. Tap a passage to play from there, or turn it into sticky notes.

iCloud sync

Your notebooks sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud account.

Folders and tags

Folders, tags, favorites and search. With Pro, search inside transcripts too.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your notes stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and transcription happens on device. Recordings are never uploaded.
