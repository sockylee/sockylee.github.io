Privacy Policy

Jotverse · iPhone, iPad, Mac · Last updated: November 26, 2026

Thank you for using Jotverse (“the App”). The App is developed and published by Shuo-Chi Li (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the App handles your data.

The short version: we have no servers, no account system, and no ads, analytics, or tracking of any kind. Your notes are stored only on your device and in your own iCloud account (handled by Apple; we cannot access them). Transcription happens on your device, and recordings are never uploaded.

1. Data we do not collect

We do not have any of the following:

  • Servers, member databases, or account systems of our own
  • Advertising, usage analytics, crash reporting, or any tracking tools
  • Any mechanism that collects your name, email, phone number, location, or other contact information

As a result, we cannot see your notebooks, images, handwriting, recordings, or transcripts.

3. iCloud sync

  • When your device is signed in to iCloud, your notebooks are stored in the App’s private database in your iCloud account (iCloud.com.sockylee.Jotverse) and sync across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Sync is handled by Apple’s iCloud. Data travels only between your devices and your iCloud account, never through us, and we cannot read it. The App does not receive your Apple Account information.
  • If you are not signed in to iCloud, your notes stay on this device only. You can also turn off iCloud for the App in the system Settings → Apple Account → iCloud.
  • Data in iCloud is handled by Apple under its Privacy Policy.

4. Recordings and transcription

  • The App does not record audio and does not use the microphone. Recordings come from your own Quick Recorder app: through the share sheet, by starting Quick Recorder from the canvas, or by opening an audio file.
  • Transcription uses Apple’s speech recognition framework and runs entirely on your device. Recordings and transcripts are never sent to us or to any server. The first time you transcribe in a language, the system downloads that language’s speech model from Apple.
  • Before transcribing, the system asks for Speech Recognition permission. You can turn it off at any time in the system Settings; other features are not affected.

5. Images, links, and export

  • Images: the App reads only the images you pick in the photo or file picker (or drag in) and stores them in the notebook. It does not need access to your photo library.
  • Link cards: when you add a link, the App connects to that website to fetch its title and preview image, just as a browser would when opening the page. We never receive the links you add.
  • Export: when you export a PNG or PDF or share a transcript, you choose where the file goes or whom to share it with.

6. In-app purchases (Jotverse Pro)

  • Payments are handled by Apple. When you buy Jotverse Pro (monthly or yearly subscription), payment takes place entirely in the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account, card, or any payment details.
  • Purchases are verified on your device. The App uses Apple’s StoreKit to check Apple-signed purchase records on your device. Purchase records are not sent to us.
  • Subscriptions, cancellations, refunds, and Family Sharing are handled by Apple under its terms. See the Terms of Use for plan details.

7. Permissions and network

  • The App asks only for Speech Recognition permission (section 4). It does not use the microphone, camera, location, or contacts.
  • The network is used only for iCloud sync (handled by the system), App Store purchases, downloading speech models (handled by the system), fetching link previews, and opening web links you tap.
  • The App does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • The App includes no third-party SDKs (no advertising, analytics, or crash reporting).

8. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-purpose note-taking tool and is not directed at children. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Because we hold none of your data, you stay in full control: delete notebooks and recordings in the App, turn off iCloud for the App in the system Settings, or delete the App to remove all local data at any time. Data in iCloud can be managed in your system’s iCloud settings.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App adds any feature that processes data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.

11. Contact
