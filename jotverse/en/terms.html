Terms of Use

Jotverse · iPhone, iPad, Mac · Last updated: November 26, 2026

The App is licensed under Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). This page adds details about the Jotverse Pro subscription and what you should know when using the App.

1. Free plan and Jotverse Pro

  • Free: unlimited notebooks; the infinite canvas with sticky notes, text, shapes, connectors, images, and links; handwriting on iPad; importing and playing audio cards; iCloud sync; PNG and PDF export; and 3 free transcriptions.
  • Jotverse Pro: unlimited transcription, automatic transcription on import, the “Text Only” and “Key Point Sticky Notes” import options, searching inside transcripts, and restoring notebooks from Recently Deleted. Pro features added in later versions are included while you are subscribed.
  • Transcription requires iOS 26, iPadOS 26, or macOS 26 or later.

2. Plans and auto-renewal

  • Jotverse Pro is offered as a monthly and a yearly subscription. Prices are shown on the in-app purchase screen and depend on your App Store country or region. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, or when a free trial ends.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription after purchase in your device’s Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or in the App under Settings → Manage Subscription. After canceling, you keep access until the end of the current period.
  • Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
  • On a new device or after reinstalling, tap “Restore Purchases” in the App.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

3. When a subscription ends

Your notebooks, recordings, and finished transcripts are never deleted and can still be opened, edited, and exported. The Pro features above are no longer available, and once the free transcriptions are used up, new recordings can’t be transcribed.

4. Your content

The notes you create, the images and recordings you import, and the transcripts you produce belong to you. The App does not upload or use your content. Please keep backups of important data. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for data loss caused by device failure, deletion, or sync issues.

5. Transcription accuracy

Transcripts are produced on device by Apple’s speech recognition and may contain errors. Accuracy depends on recording quality, accents, and language. Please check important content yourself.
